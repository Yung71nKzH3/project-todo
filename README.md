**Lava Lamp Todo** is more than just a task list. It's a mindful productivity dashboard designed to keep you focused, organized, and calm. Featuring a living, fluid background and a unified scheduling system, it's the perfect companion for your daily workflow.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📅 Unified Scheduling
*   **Integrated Calendar**: Visualize your Tasks, Events, and Subscriptions in one beautiful monthly view.
*   **Standalone Events**: Create one-off events directly on your calendar.
*   **Smart Projection**: Subscription payments are automatically projected across months based on their frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).

### 💸 Subscription Tracking
*   **Cost Management**: Track your recurring services and estimate your monthly spending.
*   **Verification System**: Track "Last Checked" dates and get alerts for outdated subscriptions.
*   **Direct Links**: One-click access to your service management pages.

### 📊 Productivity Analytics
*   **Progress Tracking**: Visual indicators of your task completion rates.
*   **Punctuality Score**: See how often you finish tasks before their due dates with our custom analytics engine.
*   **Activity Log**: A searchable, filterable timeline of your achievements and financial commitments.

### 🔔 Native Desktop Power
*   **Smart Notifications**: Native alerts at 1 week, 1 day, 1 hour, and 30 minutes before deadlines.
*   **Morning & Mid-day Reminders**: Automatic "Good Morning" and "Check-in" alerts at 9 AM and 1 PM on due dates.
*   **Launch on Startup**: Configure the app to start with your computer so you're always on top of your day.

### 🎨 Premium Aesthetics
*   **Glassmorphic UI**: Floating cards over a soothing, animated lava lamp background.
*   **Adaptive Theming**: Full Dark and Light mode support with calibrated contrast for maximum readability.
*   **Fluid Animations**: Powered by Framer Motion for a life-like feel.

## 🚀 Installation

### Windows
1.  Go to the [Releases](../../releases) page.
2.  Download the latest `.exe` installer.
3.  Run the installer and enjoy!

### Linux (Fedora/RPM)
1.  Download the `.rpm` package from the [Releases](../../releases) page.
2.  Install via DNF:
    ```bash
    sudo dnf install ./lava-lamp-todo_1.0.0_x86_64.rpm
    ```

## 🛠️ Development

Built with **React 19**, **Tauri v2**, **Tailwind CSS v4**, and **Framer Motion**.

### Prerequisites
*   Node.js (v20+)
*   Rust (v1.77+)

### Setup
1.  `npm install`
2.  `npm run dev` (Web view)
3.  `npm run tauri dev` (Desktop app)
